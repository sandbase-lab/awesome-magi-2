# MAGI-2 model comparison

English · [简体中文](zh-CN/model-comparison.md) · [← Back to the collection](../README.md)

Two kinds of evidence, kept apart:

1. **What we measured.** Lab measurements of MAGI-2, dated.
2. **What creators reported.** Posts on X with their own prompts and claims. Useful context, not verified by us.

MAGI-2 was announced on November 24, 2026. This list records no measured result for it yet, so the first section is empty. The second section comes from the creator posts in this collection, most of them from launch week.

## What we measured

**No MAGI-2 measurement is recorded in this list yet.** The Lab Evals boards this list links to rank language models, and the Pelican Test asks for an SVG drawing. Neither measures video output directly, so neither can place MAGI-2 yet. This list also has no measured rival results for video models to show beside it.

When Lab records a measurement for MAGI-2, this section gets a dated row and the claims below get revisited in a normal PR.

What this says, and what it doesn't:

- **The only cost claim is relative.** The 1080p comparison says "much cheaper" with no numbers. No creator in this list states what they paid.
- **Several posts are partner posts.** Five of the posts that shared a prompt tag Higgsfield with #HiggsfieldPartner. Read them as showcases from a partner, not neutral tests.
- **Launch week means one run each.** Most posts are a single attempt on launch day, with no shared method.

## What it costs on SandBase

The list price from SandBase is 0.08 per second of duration, with a default of 5 seconds, so a default clip is 0.40 (per SandBase inspect, `sandai/magi-2/text-to-video`). This is the list price, not a measurement of what any creator paid.
